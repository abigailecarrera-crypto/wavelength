How to play
  1. Each round shows a spectrum — two opposite ideas, like Cold ↔ Hot.
  2. The Psychic lifts the screen and sees where the hidden target landed, then lowers it again.
  3. The Psychic types one clue that sits at that exact spot on the spectrum.
  4. The Guesser moves the dial to where they think the target is and locks it in.
  5. The screen swings open. You score together — the bullseye is worth the most.
  6. Roles swap each round. You're playing as a team against your own best score.
